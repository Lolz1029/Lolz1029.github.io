Most models fail due to being too complex for its purpose. As depicted in [[On Exactitude in Science by Jorge Luis Borges]], a 1:1 map of the Earth is useless for navigation as it provides no abstraction. Implement the [[80:20 Rule]] to create a models that are simple, relevant, and powerful.

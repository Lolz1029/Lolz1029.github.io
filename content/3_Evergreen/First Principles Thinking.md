One can easily dig into the first principles of an argument by repeatedly asking "why", as [[Elon Musk by Walter Isaacson]] did when building batteries for Tesla. Good arguments are grounded in solid first principles—falsifiable facts that have stood the test of public scrutiny. This is contrary to analogous thinking, which requires less cognitive load but seldom leads to innovation.

A model is not reality — it is a simplification of reality.
"I've always done it this way" or "That's just how it is" are red flags of people:
- cutting feedback loops.
- looking at the map as reality.
So always ask myself: when has this map been updated?
> All models are wrong; the practical question is how wrong do they have to be to not be useful
> – George Box

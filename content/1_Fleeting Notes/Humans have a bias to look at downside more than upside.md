Under equal probability, people on average accept risk if the upside is 2.5X the downside. Understand that fear makes you biased towards the downside when investing resources. Ways to mitigate this fear are to:
- go back to first principles; why is it a downside? You will find that most times there isn't as much downside as you thought [[Everything stems from a first principle]].
- play long term games with a high average win-rate [[Amateurs think in absolutes. Experts think in probabilities]].

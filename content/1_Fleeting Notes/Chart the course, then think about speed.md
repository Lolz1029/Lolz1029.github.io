"Do not automate until the process is complete" -Elon Musk
Progress is a two-step process: orientation and speed. Speed does not matter without orientation and vice-versa, however, orientation is most important. 
Expending energy generates speed, but speed can be stifled by your own inertia or outside mediums such as the viscocity or friction [[Inertia is the enemy of execution]]. Remember that not all energy input creates the same output. Using energy to change the environment's friction and viscocity is key to making strides towards the things you want to get done.

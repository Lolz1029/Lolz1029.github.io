People who break out of linear growth think in terms of first principles. First principles are falsifiable facts that have stood the test of public scrutiny — commonly known as truths. 
Many people think by analogy:
- "Because it's always been this way"
- "This is how it is"
The socratic method and 5 why's are techniques to dig into the core assumptions of arguments to seek the first principles, and operate completely differently than others.

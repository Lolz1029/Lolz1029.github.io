Game theory suggests that the best way to win in long term games comes from two factors:
- Leading with positivity
- Reciprocating their response, with a hint of forgiveness from time to time.
Do not wait for others to engage in the game — always be the one to initiate positive interaction. After this, reciprocate their attitude towards you and do not be a pushover. However, one must not forget to forgive every once in a while, as this helps escape constant negative loops.

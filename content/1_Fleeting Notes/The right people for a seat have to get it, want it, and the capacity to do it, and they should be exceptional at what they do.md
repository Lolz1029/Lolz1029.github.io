Once organizations have established the seats of the organization, they start to fill in the seats using GWC. How can I make the accountability chart before we decide the company culture? The answer is — if it is not evident that they are a good fit for the company, then they should not be on the leadership team. 
- Do they thrive in your culture?
- Have they made the organization better?
If the reality is that there is no one who can take full accountability for a seat, then you need to seek talent.

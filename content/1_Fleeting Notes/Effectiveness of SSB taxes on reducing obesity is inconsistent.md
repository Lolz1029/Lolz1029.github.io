Analysis on cities implementing SSB taxes showed a 1.64% decrease in BMI for the youth, and decreases of ~4% for children under 11 [[Deborah Young_2024_oi_240781_1721767291.32208.pdf]]. However, there was no statistically significant reduction in overall obesity.

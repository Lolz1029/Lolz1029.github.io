Standing in someone else's shoes to assess a situation is something we are taught from when we are young. Many adults struggle with executing on this concept. Everything you experince is subjective: everything happens relative to you. The only way to become objective is by considering multiple viewpoints and seeing the situation from multiple angles; what would they think? [[Invert everything]]
When hiring, don't just look from the company's perspective. Understanding the applicant's views lead to long-term wins [[Make everything black and white when hiring for an easier firing]]

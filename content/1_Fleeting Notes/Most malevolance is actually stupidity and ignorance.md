[[If two solutions point towards the same result, opt for the simpler one]] The simplest explanation for a person's actions is more likely to be correct — intentional action occures less than you think. Things that seem like calculated malintentions are most of the time just coincedental stupidity. 

The driver did not risk their life to make you slam your brakes, and the grocery shop did not try to scam you; the driver didn't see you and someone at the grocery market just forgot to take off the 50% off tag off the shelves.

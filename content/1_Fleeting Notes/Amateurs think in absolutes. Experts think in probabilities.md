#probability #decisionmaking 
Two truths from Baysian thinking: 
1. Everything is a probability
2. Probabilities are constantly updated
If we do not understand the base rate, new information does not mean anything. For example, "kids in this neighborhood are 2 times more likely to be abducted" means nothing on its own if we do not know the base rate: what is the probability that a kid gets abducted? The answer is 1/1,000,000 in the USA. So this statement means we are at two in a million.

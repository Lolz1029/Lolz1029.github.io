Everything heads towards an equilibrium. Whether that is people in companies, friends, or society, the more people are allowed to connect with eachother, the more they become similar. If you do not desire equilibrium, then insulators need to be put in place — however, insulators are expensive to maintain and they are only preventing the inevitable. 
- The Berlin Wall and The Great Wall of China are examples of this. The walls took lots of resources to create and maintain, but they could not stop the ideologies from merging.

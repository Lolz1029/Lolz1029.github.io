Entropy increases naturally; If noone expends energy to create order in this world, the world around us naturally heads towards chaos. From your room to governments, if there is no consistent force that is maintaining order within closed systems, things will become messy. Look for areas where there is noone actively decreasing entropy, as they will become chaotic as time passes.
There are efficient ways to decrease entropy, and not all work is equal outcome.

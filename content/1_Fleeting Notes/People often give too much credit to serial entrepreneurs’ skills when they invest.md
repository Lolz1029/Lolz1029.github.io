When someone has been successful before, investors tend to assume they’ll succeed again — this is a mental shortcut (called a **simplifying heuristic**) that simplifies decision-making.
Because of this bias, an entrepreneur’s reputation might boost their perceived value more than their actual ability justifies — leading to **amplified performance expectations**.

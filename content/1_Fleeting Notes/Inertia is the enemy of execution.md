Things with more mass are harder to stop. This concept explains the behavior of may individuals. People born into a certain belief will keep it, and will for the most part be unaware of it. Even if you are able to think logically and know what course to take, inertia makes it difficult for people to accept change and make progress — doing nothing is easier than doing something [[Chart the course, then think about speed]].

#technology 
People have a tendency to see growth as something that is linear, where in reality it is not. It comes up suddenly, so people have to be aware of how the world can change in a matter of months. A great example of this is technology — who would have imagined in 2022 that they would be able to have human conversations with their phones? In the last 5 years, we have progressed technologically than our progress in the last 20. Experts are saying that "I have never seen this in my life before". That is a sign of their model being broken by the reality of exponential growth. [[A model is not reality]]

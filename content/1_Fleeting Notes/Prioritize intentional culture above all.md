#culture #autonomy
 [[If you hire on skill alone and dismiss core values, you will not retain talent]]. Great culture is not a "good to have" but a must. This is because culture directly affects the people in the company, and the people are what drive everything.
- Command and control culture instills fear, which turns employees into mere cogs in a machine [[Fear inhibits creativity and autonomy]].
- Happy accident cultures do not scale.
- Intentional culture creates autonomy and scales.
The best company is one where everyone is doing what they love and are very good at doing.

- The emergence of ideas are only possible in a non-linear plane of thinking, as it is impossible to plan for a discovery. To make new connections in thought, you need an environment for ideas to connect freely.
- Self-sustainable work has to be self-rewarding. External rewards to the system will seldom result in a long-term solution, and never a maintenance free solution.
- The ability to create new nodes in our mind may decrease with age, but the ability to hang ideas from a "latticework of mental models" may be a different story.
- Growth comes from personal experience + history. You cannot just learn the techniques and copy as that would be a performance. It is also inefficient to invent the wheel every time you start a project—therefore it is vital to find the goldilocks zone. I tend to overweight on preparing and planning instead of doing, so I should realize this fact.
- To achieve a flow state, I need to relieve my mind of small tasks. I don't need to complete the tasks but I have to convince my mind that they do not require my attention. A way to do this is to write the task down in the eisenhower matrix or capturing it.
- People tend to associate familiarity with comprehension. People also like things that are familiar as well.
